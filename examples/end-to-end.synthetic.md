# End-to-end synthetic walkthrough

**Entirely fictional.** This walkthrough does not describe a real organization, person, location, campaign, operating result or private system.

## Scenario

A fictional refreshment brand wants two sample stores to introduce the same new product while preserving different customer occasions. Store A supports quick neighborhood visits. Store B supports guided discovery with friends. The shared direction is confirmed, but no pricing, publishing or external-system action is authorized.

## Step 1 — Strategy

Use `blackshark-brand-store-strategy` to connect the shared objective with different store roles.

Candidate result:

- Keep one clear product story across both stores.
- Let Store A emphasize fast recognition and a short service flow.
- Let Store B emphasize guided comparison and a planned social occasion.
- Protect Store A's speed and Store B's staffing capacity as explicit constraints.

This is a proposal based on supplied context, not evidence that customers will respond as expected.

## Step 2 — Validation

Use `blackshark-operating-validation` to define proportionate evidence:

- readiness evidence: product information and staff guidance are available;
- customer evidence: whether people understand the offer and choose the intended occasion;
- operating evidence: service time, staffing load and incremental costs;
- conclusion limit: readiness can be assessed before commercial outcomes are known.

No additional meeting or repeated approval is required for the already-confirmed shared direction.

## Step 3 — Action planning

Use `blackshark-action-planning` to keep the complete dependency set:

1. Prepare the shared product explanation.
2. Adapt the first customer touchpoint for each store role.
3. Prepare quick-service guidance for Store A.
4. Prepare guided-discovery guidance for Store B.
5. Confirm required staff capacity.
6. Record the evidence to collect.
7. Define the review point and owner.

A priority view may highlight the first three items, but it does not delete the remaining dependencies.

## Step 4 — Handoff

Use `blackshark-project-handoff` to prepare a collaborative handoff containing the purpose, store-specific work, owners, dependencies, evidence expectations and review point. Unknown owners block only the tasks that require them; completed or independent work can continue.

No external action is performed. The walkthrough produces text only.

## Step 5 — Review

Assume the fictional team later reports that both stores completed the planned setup, but only service-time observations were collected.

Use `blackshark-operating-review` to conclude:

- execution: the setup was completed;
- customer response: unknown because it was not observed;
- store economics: unknown because incremental costs and outcomes were not measured;
- method reliability: still a candidate because the expected customer behavior was not tested;
- next state: continue only the evidence collection needed for the original question.

Commercial effectiveness remains unknown. Completion of the plan is not treated as proof of customer or business impact.
