# Strategy relationship model

Use the smallest useful slice of this model:

1. Purpose/asset: what choice, behavior, relationship, or capability should become easier or stronger?
2. Store role: what does each store contribute, for which occasion, customer, time, product, or experience?
3. Journey: what must the customer understand, choose, experience, and remember; which product, channel, platform, staff, or space carries each step?
4. Trade-off: what people, budget, supply, time, attention, or store capacity is consumed; which project is explicitly not prioritized?
5. Evidence: what behavior or result would support the assumption, from which source, over what window, and what would remain unknowable?

For shared work, check four relationships explicitly: brand→store support, store→brand evidence, store↔store role/transfer effects, and brand project↔store operation resource/cost conflicts. Preserve an unaffected store's approved work and allow light or no participation when the shared project would damage its operating base.

Keep business results and reusable method/capability evidence in separate accounts. A store result does not automatically prove a brand result or cross-store repeatability.
