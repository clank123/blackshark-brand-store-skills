# Scenario routing

- Opening: use the store's intended role, customer occasion, service capacity, and minimum evidence; do not treat launch readiness as proof of demand.
- Ongoing operations: start from the current bottleneck and existing decisions; do not rebuild a brand plan for a small confirmed task.
- Product or campaign node: connect the product promise to distinct store roles and the customer journey; identify shared resources and evidence windows.
- Repeat visits or relationship: specify the behavior that counts as return or recommendation; do not substitute orders, revenue, or exposure without a defined relationship measure.
- Structural adjustment: separate expression, transaction, store-model, and brand-level hypotheses; revisit only the layer whose evidence changed.
- Multi-store work: state each store's role, transfer risk, shared dependencies, and what would count as network-level evidence.
