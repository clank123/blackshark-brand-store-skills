# BlackShark Operating Validation

Turn an operating direction into a project that can be approved, executed, evidenced, reviewed, and either continued or stopped. This is the general core; a brand-specific or team-specific adapter may supply facts and interfaces, but this Skill does not require a private repository or collaboration platform.

## Input gate

Identify only what the current decision needs: current facts and problem, hypothesis, target user and behavior, candidate actions, owner or existing authority, time window, constraints, evidence sources, and explicit non-goals. For complex work also state time level, store stage/role, structural bottleneck, main asset direction, and relationship to other projects or nodes. If an item is missing, isolate the affected conclusion or execution step and continue the rest of the requested deliverable; do not replace a usable proposal with a gap list.

## Contract and gates

Use a stable project ID only when the work genuinely needs a validation project. Keep its relevant contract fields together: facts/problem, hypothesis, target behavior, actions, evidence contract, applicable gates, continuation and stop conditions, result, next decision, and business/capability assets. The evidence contract names only the metrics needed for the decision, with meaning, source, baseline or comparison, window, collector, confidence, limitations, fallback evidence, and conclusions that are not allowed. A simple confirmed task or low-risk reversible change can use its existing handoff and evidence note instead of a formal project.

Treat supplied confirmed decisions and delegated authority as inherited, not as new approval requests. Human decision is required only for a materially new or changed direction, hypothesis adoption, resource commitment, owner, external release, final business result, or method promotion that is not already covered by that authority. Gates are decision criteria, not automatic meetings or repeated approvals: apply an already-approved gate when its evidence arrives, and ask only when a new choice exceeds current authority. If one store or project changes, do not ask the owner of an unaffected approved part to reconfirm that it can continue; state the observable operating condition instead. AI may retrieve, compare, draft, and check, but must not turn an unconfirmed item into a decision or write a weak judgment as fact.

## Result and next decision

Allowed results: `成立`, `部分成立`, `不成立`, `无法判断`. Allowed next decisions: `加码`, `保持`, `调整`, `停止`, `补证据`. Keep result and decision separate. Publication, exposure, task completion, or a green workflow only proves an action occurred; without the purchase-to-realization and cost chain, do not output formal ROI.

Always report three conclusions separately:

- Readiness: whether inputs, capacity, responsibility and safeguards are sufficient to run the next step.
- Business effect: what observed customer and operating evidence supports, does not support, or cannot yet determine.
- Method reliability: whether the approach has repeated real samples, independent operation, boundaries and counterexamples.

Prospective assumptions are not observed results, and an analytical conclusion is not a human decision. Missing commercial evidence limits the business-effect conclusion only; it does not erase a supported readiness judgment. One successful store or one cycle does not promote a method.

Read `references/validation-contract.md` for proportional contract shapes. Complete the requested strategy or validation deliverable within current authority; do not output only a next-hop recommendation.
